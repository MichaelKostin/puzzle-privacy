For questions, technical issues, purchase help, or feedback, email support@kostin.app. Please include your device type and app version when reporting a technical problem. Do not include payment-card details or passwords.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock all puzzles?
An adult can open the parent area and choose the option to unlock all puzzles. Available plans and prices appear on the subscription screen.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open the parent area, go to Settings, and choose “Restore purchases.” Use the same Apple or Google account that made the original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?
Use the subscription settings in your Apple App Store or Google Play account. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Is Puzzle Garden safe for children?
The app has no advertising, accounts, social features, or behavioral analytics. Purchase controls are behind a parental gate.
